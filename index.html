01 — ABOUT ME

Creative by nature.
Strategic by design.

I enjoy the space where creativity meets communication — turning a brand’s message into content people actually want to watch, read and remember.

My experience spans social media promotion, content creation, digital marketing, website projects and visual communication. I’ve worked on presenting products, shaping online brand presence and creating digital experiences.

I like to understand the audience first, find the story worth telling, then build the content around it. The goal is always to make the work feel intentional — not just fill a feed.
