About me

I build digital work with clarity.

I’m Hitin, a web designer and creative developer based in Dubai. I care about thoughtful interfaces, strong visual direction and digital experiences that feel simple to use.

The person behind the work

Design should feel intentional.

My work sits between design and development, with attention to the details that make a page feel finished.

Less noise. More meaning.

I like turning rough ideas into clear, polished experiences. I’m drawn to bold typography, purposeful motion, useful structure and honest communication.

A practical creative process.

I start by understanding what matters, shape a visual direction around it, then build the interface with care across every screen.
